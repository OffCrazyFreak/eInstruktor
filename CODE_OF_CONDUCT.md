# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want e-instruktor to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give helpful, actionable feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Discriminatory, sexual, or violent language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any official communication channels) and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer,
**Jakov Jakovac** (via his [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jakov-jakovac/)).
All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

The maintainer may take any action deemed appropriate, including warning the
offender, removing comments or contributions, or banning the individual from the
project temporarily or permanently.
